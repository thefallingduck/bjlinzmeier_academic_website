This is the personal research website of Benjamin J. Linzmeier.

## Analytics (only with your consent)

If you click **Accept** on the cookie banner, this site loads Google Analytics, which sets cookies (such as `_ga`) in your browser to count visits and see which pages are read. IP addresses are anonymized. Nothing from Google Analytics loads if you click **Decline** or ignore the banner.

You can change your choice at any time with the **Privacy settings** link at the bottom of every page. Declining removes the Google Analytics cookies from this site.

## Other things this site stores or loads

- Your light/dark mode preference is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.
- Page fonts are served from this site itself, not from Google or any other third party.
- If you use a contact form on this site, the information you type (such as your name, email address, who you are, and your message) is collected by this site's host, Netlify, and emailed to me. I use them only to reply to you.
- Links to other websites (publishers, ResearchGate, etc.) are governed by those sites' own policies.
